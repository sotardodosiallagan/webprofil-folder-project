Deskripsi Diri

I'm an Informatics Engineering student at Universitas Negeri Surabaya (UNESA) and a recipient of the AMN Surabaya Scholarship. I'm currently building my foundation in programming and computer systems, and I enjoy design because it helps me think about how people actually use technology.

Before university, I competed in choir at the provincial through international level, including a Silver A Award at the Malaysia Choral Eisteddfod, and served as a church organist. Those years taught me discipline, teamwork, and how to perform under pressure, and I bring the same habits to my studies.

I'm looking to learn, collaborate on projects, and connect with people in software and tech.
